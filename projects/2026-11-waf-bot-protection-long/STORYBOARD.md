# STORYBOARD · AWS WAF Bot Protection: Bot, DDoS and Layered Defense

Theme `cleanui`, format `16x9` 1920x1080, YouTube long-form, Vietnamese voice-over (VieNeu-TTS, voice Hải Đăng, `assets/audio/voiceover.mp3`, 725.55 s).
Sources: `SCRIPT.md` (146 spoken lines, 11 chapters), `SCRIPT.source.md` (visual notes), `scripts/plan-beats.py` SCENES (47 scenes).
Alignment: asr.py medium, 94.1% exact syllable match, 99.3% matched; `vo-alignment.json` and `syl-times.json` are the timing source. The table times are recorded seconds from `vo-alignment.json`.
Planning language is English; on-screen text follows the SCRIPT.md chapter headings (English) and product names.

## Rules for this film

- Every scene uses the caption band `mid` (captions at y 980). Content stays in x 160-1760, y 100-900.
- Logos: only the official files in `assets/logos/aws/` and `assets/logos/goc-nhin-dev-logo.png`. No drawn or generated logos. No photos: `assets/img/` is empty for this film, so every picture is a cleanui card built from HTML/CSS and these icons.
- Feature names without their own logo file (Anti-DDoS Managed Rule Group, Rate-based Rules, Common and Targeted Protection, Count mode, Origin Protection) are shown as text labels on the closest official mark: `waf.svg`, `waf-rule.svg`, `waf-bot-control.svg`, `shield-advanced.svg`.
- No AWS console screenshot. Every dashboard is a cleanui card drawn to look like a dashboard; it shows no real data.
- Not all bots are blocked: Googlebot is ALLOW (c2b, c7e), bad scrapers are LIMIT or BLOCK, suspicious requests get CAPTCHA or browser challenge (c7d, c8c).
- Chapter opener: the first scene of each chapter starts with a chapter card for about 3.5 s (number in accent, uppercase title from SCRIPT.md, cleanui mascot and starburst), then the scene content fades in.
- Every scene changes at least every 3 to 5 s; a scene holds no more than 3 s still.
- Payoff and sound: the sentence 142-144 tail uses no riser; the channel line (145-146) is the only spoken brand statement before the end card.
- End card: after the last spoken syllable plus 0.30 s, from `plan-beats.py` (`ec`), the house end card in build.py with the text "Góc nhìn DEV - AWS: Behind the Cloud. Hẹn gặp bạn ở tập tiếp theo!".
